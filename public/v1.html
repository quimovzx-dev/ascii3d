ASCII 3D

Give it photos, get a 3D model drawn in ASCII. Drag to rotate. Everything runs in your browser; photos are never uploaded.

Pick a photo to start. A plain background works best.
Honest limits. With one photo the back and sides are guessed (the shape is inflated from the outline and, optionally, AI depth, then mirrored). With several photos taken evenly around the object (turntable style, first photo = front, going around in one direction, same distance and height) it carves a real shape from the silhouettes. Hollows and concave parts cannot be recovered from silhouettes.
drag to rotate - scroll or pinch to zoom
